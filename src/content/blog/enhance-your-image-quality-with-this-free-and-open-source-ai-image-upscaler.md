## Upscayle Features

Here are Upscayl features to support your creative work.

#### **Free and Open Source**

Upscayl is free and open source, means that you can see all its original source code. You also can build new features or customize on top of original application by your own. Because of open source, you can use the application for free without any subscriptions and worrying about data privacy. Your data will be saved and not be shared by others. This will make you more convenience to enhance your images.

#### **Save Image in High Quality**

Upscayl let you save your low quality image to the higher one up to 16x. This is best for you to enhance some low quality images that you have without missing its detail with the help of AI models.&#x20;

#### **Batch Upscayl**

If you have a number of images to being enhanced at once, you can use Upscayl batch feature. So you don’t need to enhance your image one by one. This will save your time and your work.

#### **Model for Every Need**

Upscayl provides some models that you can choose based on your need. You can try these models to get best result of your images. But some of models can be run properly depend on your computer spesifications. The better model also need some times to wait for the result depend on computer spesification and image size. If you don’t have powerfull computer, you still can use Upscayl with the default model or you can try Upscayl Cloud.
